Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Habit Flow (عاداتك) mobile application for Android, published by NOVU.

Last updated: September 2026

Habit Flow is a habit-tracking app that runs fully offline. Your habit data never leaves your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what this means in practice.

1. Overview

Habit Flow stores all of your data — habits, check-ins, streaks, XP and statistics — in a local database on your own device. The app does not require an account, does not use cloud storage, and does not connect to any server operated by NOVU or any third party.

2. What we collect

Nothing that leaves your device. Habit data, check-ins and progress live in a local database on your phone. No accounts, no sign-up telemetry, no third-party SDKs, and no personal data (such as name, email or phone number) is collected or transmitted.

3. How we use your data

Your entries power only the app's own screens: habit lists, streaks, statistics and the heatmap. They are never transmitted, shared, sold or analysed by anyone else. The app works the same with airplane mode on.

4. Data retention & deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can delete an individual habit or wipe all of your data at any time with one tap. Removing the app from your device permanently deletes its local database.

5. Your rights

Following standard privacy-law principles, you can export a complete copy of your data at any time, in a readable format, and take it anywhere. You may also request deletion of your data by contacting NOVU using the details at the end of this policy.

6. Security measures

Your data stays inside a private, app-scoped storage area, protected by your device operating system and your screen lock. Because no data is transmitted, there is nothing to intercept in transit.

7. Third parties

There are none. No analytics services, advertisement networks or crash-reporting services are integrated, and no data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated only if a future version of the app ever handles data differently. Any change will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact NOVU through the NOVU Studio.
