About the artist

( 02 / Story )

Doha creates expressive characters and atmospheric worlds — art that carries a feeling before it carries a subject.

Working across digital media, Doha turns loose ideas into character studies, concept art and editorial illustrations. Every piece starts with a mood — light, temperature, story — and is built brush by brush until the image feels alive.
