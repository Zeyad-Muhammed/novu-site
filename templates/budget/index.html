FAQ

Which apps does it work in?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, Google Sheets (File → Import, or open from Drive) and LibreOffice Calc. Numbers on Mac opens it too, with simpler charts.

Do I need to know formulas?

No. You only type in the tinted cells — everything else calculates itself. Each file opens on a Start Here sheet with the steps.

Is my data private?

Yes. It's a file on your own device or Drive. There is no account, no sync and nothing sent to NOVU.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, through the Google Sheets or Excel mobile apps.
