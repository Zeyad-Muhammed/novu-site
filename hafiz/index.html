WHY OFFLINE

Most password managers live in the cloud.
Yours doesn't have to.

A cloud vault is a target — a server somewhere holding the key to your entire digital life. Hafiz takes the opposite path: the app holds nothing, sends nothing, and knows nothing. It simply opens a vault that lives on your device, and closes it again the moment you look away.

01

No account, no sync

There is no Hafiz account, no cloud sync, no recovery email, no server-side copy of anything. The concept of "an account" doesn't exist.

02

No permission, no leak

The Android build declares no INTERNET permission at all. Not a single byte of your vault can be sent anywhere — it is structurally impossible.

03

No key at rest

Your master password becomes a key through 100,000 PBKDF2 rounds. That key exists only in memory, and is wiped the instant the vault locks.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is the vault really offline? How can that be?

The Android release declares no INTERNET permission. Without that permission Android blocks all network access at the system level, so the app physically cannot send your data — there's no sync server, no account, and no recovery email to abuse.

How do I install Hafiz without Google Play?

Download the signed APK from the download section, allow installation from this source when Android asks, and install. It's our direct version 1.2.0 build, and you can verify it against the published SHA-256. A Google Play release is coming.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Nothing can recover it — that's the point of a vault. The key is derived from your password and never stored. The free plan includes encrypted backups and emergency access so you're never one password away from losing everything.

How does emergency access work without a server?

Your trusted contact installs Hafiz and opens your emergency grant. After the waiting period you set, they generate time-limited codes on their own device, verify them locally, and read your instructions — all offline, peer-verified, no server involved.

What exactly does the duress password do?

Typing a duress password opens a separate decoy vault containing accounts you choose to place there. It looks like a normal vault to anyone watching — but it was unlocked with its own key and the real vault was never touched.

Can I move from my current password manager?

Yes. Hafiz imports CSV exports from Chrome, Bitwarden, LastPass and KeePass, skipping duplicates. You can also transfer individual accounts between devices with encrypted QR codes.

What does the free plan limit? Is it a trial?

Free is free, not a trial. It's limited to 25 accounts — a generous ceiling for most people. Hafiz Pro removes the cap entirely for $20/month and funds the product's future.

Can I keep my vault on more than one device?

There is no cloud sync by design. Move accounts with encrypted QR transfer, or restore a full encrypted backup file on another device — hand-carried, never cloud-carried.
