## Callouts, tables and find & replace

          
Note, Warning and Tip callouts stand out in the preview. Tables get a small toolbar to add rows and columns or align text. Find & replace supports match case, whole word and regex.

          

        


        

          
## A status for every note

          
Mark notes Active, On hold, Done or Dropped, pin the important ones, add tags and nest notebooks as deep as you like. Select many notes at once to move, tag or delete them.

          

            Active
            On hold
            Done
            Dropped
          

          

        


        

          
## Nothing gets lost

          
Every save keeps a revision. Open History to see exactly what changed, line by line, and restore any version. Your current text is kept as a copy first.

          

        


        

          
## Sync with your own Git repo

          
Point Dev Notes at a private repository and it syncs after saving or every few minutes, using the Git sign-in you already have. No accounts, no servers of ours. Backups and export are built in too.

        04 / files
        
Your notes are just files.

        
Dev Notes saves every note as a normal Markdown file in your Documents folder. Open them in any editor, back them up however you like, and keep them if you ever stop using the app.
